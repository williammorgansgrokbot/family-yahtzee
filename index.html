HOW TO PLAY

Each turn you get up to 3 rolls. Tap dice to hold them between rolls. Then tap one open box on your scorecard and tap SCORE – you must fill a box every turn, even with 0 (a scratch). After 13 rounds every box is full and the highest total wins. Upper section 63+ earns a 35 bonus. Each extra Yahtzee after scoring 50 earns +100 and works as a joker: use the matching upper box if open, otherwise any lower box at full value (Full House 25, Straights 30/40).
